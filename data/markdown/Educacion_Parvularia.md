**Corrección aplicada.** El texto publicado en la fuente conflictiva era: «Manifestar interés por canciones, juegos y bailes que forman parte de su cotidianeidad.». curriculumnacional.cl publishes the statement of OA 03 CES SC twice: under OA 03 and again under OA 04, both on the núcleo listing and on each objective's own detail page. The duplication is in the ministry's HTML, not in this project's parser - the extractor reproduces the page faithfully, and both pages were re-fetched to confirm it. The Bases Curriculares de Educación Parvularia, which the site itself publishes as the governing document, print five distinct objectives for the núcleo Comprensión del Entorno Sociocultural in the Primer Nivel (Sala Cuna); OA 3 is the canciones/juegos/bailes objective and OA 4 is the utensilios domésticos one.
